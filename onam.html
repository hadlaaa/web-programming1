ONAM

Onam is the most spectacular, culturally rich harvest festival of Kerala, celebrated with immense joy, unity, and grandeur by Keralites all over the world regardless of their religion. Lasting for ten vibrant days during the Malayalam month of Chingam (falling between August and September), Onam marks the annual homecoming of the legendary and beloved mythical ruler, King Mahabali. According to legend, Kerala witnessed its golden age under Mahabali's righteous, egalitarian rule, where everyone lived in perfect equality, honesty, and prosperity. This prosperous reign challenged the supremacy of the gods, leading Lord Vishnu to take the avatar of Vamana (a dwarf Brahmin) to test the king's humility and ultimately send him to the underworld, but granting him a single boon: the right to visit his people once every year. To assure their ancient king that they are still happy and prosperous, the people of Kerala transform the state into a breathtaking spectacle of lights, flowers, and cultural art.
